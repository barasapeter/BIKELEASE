01 — How It Works

FOUR STEPS
TO PAYMENT

From customer arrival to settled payment — BikeLease handles every step, whether you're on the web dashboard or dialing from any basic phone.

01

Customer Arrives

Open a new lease via the dashboard or USSD menu. Select a bike and log the customer's details in seconds.

02

Clock Starts

Confirm "Start Lease." The live timer begins and runs in the background — close the app or walk away, tracking continues.

03

Customer Returns

Stop the lease. Elapsed minutes × RPM = amount due. Calculated instantly, no mental math, no disputes.

04

Collect Payment

Record cash on the spot or trigger an M-Pesa STK Push to the customer's phone. Revenue logs automatically.

02 — USSD Access

WORKS ON
ANY PHONE

No smartphone. No app. No data connection. BikeLease's USSD interface gives operators full lease control from any basic handset — built for outdoor markets, roadside stands, and areas with limited connectivity.

Simple Number Menus

Every action is one keypress. Dial the code, select a number, confirm — lease started. Zero learning curve, maximum productivity.

Start & Stop in Under 10 Seconds

Open or close an active lease from any phone. Elapsed time and billing amount confirmed on screen before the session ends.

Live Business Data Anywhere

Pull today's revenue, check fleet availability, and view pending leases — all through a single USSD session, from any location.

PIN-Protected & Always Synced

Every USSD action is operator PIN-protected and syncs instantly to the same cloud ledger as the web dashboard. One source of truth.

04 — Pricing Model

THE RPM
FORMULA

Rate Per Minute is the most honest way to price bike rentals. Customers pay for exactly what they use — no rounding to the nearest hour, no checkout disputes.

Set the RPM per bike. BikeLease handles the rest — every session, from web or USSD, with zero manual calculation.

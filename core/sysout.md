> “What useful outputs, reports, and insights does this system give the business owner?”

Let’s break it down properly.

---

# 1. Daily Operational Reports

These help the owner run the shop day-to-day.

### a) Daily Revenue Report

* Total money earned today
* Breakdown by:

  * Bike type
  * Payment method (cash, mobile money, card)
* Number of rentals today

Owner uses this to know: *Did we make profit today?*

---

### b) Active Rentals Report

* List of bikes currently leased
* Student name
* Registration number
* Start time
* Expected return time (if applicable)

👉 Helps the owner track:

* Which bikes are out
* Who has them
* If any are overdue

---

### c) Overdue Bikes Report

* Student details
* Bike ID
* Time overdue
* Estimated penalty

👉 Very important for enforcing rules and preventing losses.

---

# 2. Financial Reports

These help the owner understand money trends over time.

###  a) Weekly / Monthly Revenue Summary

* Total revenue
* Revenue by bike type
* Most profitable bike category

👉 Helps answer:

* Which bikes make the most money?
* Should I buy more of a certain type?

---

###  b) Profit Estimation Report

If you include:

* Maintenance costs
* Staff wages
* Shop rent

Then system can generate:

* Gross revenue
* Expenses
* Net profit

This makes your system more “business intelligent”.

---

# 3. Bike Utilization Reports

This is very impressive for a supervisor.

###  a) Bike Usage Report

* How many times each bike was rented
* Total hours used per bike
* Revenue generated per bike

👉 Owner learns:

* Which bikes are popular?
* Which bikes are rarely used?

---

###  b) Idle Bike Report

* Bikes that haven’t been rented in X days

👉 Might indicate:

* Pricing problem
* Bike condition issue

---

# 4. Customer (Student) Reports

###  a) Frequent Customers Report

* Students who rent most often
* Total amount spent
* Total hours rented

👉 Owner can:

* Create loyalty rewards
* Offer discounts

---

###  b) Blacklist / Risk Report

* Students who:

  * Return bikes late repeatedly
  * Fail to pay on time
  * Damage bikes

Very important for risk management.

---

# 5. Payment Reports

###  a) Payment Method Distribution

* % cash
* % mobile money
* % card

👉 Helps owner decide:

* Which payment systems to prioritize

---

###  b) Pending Payments Report

If you allow pay-later or penalties.

---

# 6. Inventory Reports

###  a) Bike Inventory Status

* Total bikes
* Available bikes
* Rented bikes
* Under maintenance

---

###  b) Maintenance Report

* Bikes needing service
* Last service date
* Maintenance history
* Cost of maintenance

👉 Prevents breakdown losses.

---

# 7. Receipts & Documents Generated

The system should generate:

* Rental receipt
* Payment receipt
* Lease agreement record
* Daily summary sheet (printable)
* Monthly financial statement (PDF export)


---

# 8. Analytics & Forecasting (Advanced)


### The system could generate:

* Peak rental hours (e.g., 8–10 AM)
* Peak rental days (e.g., Mondays)
* Revenue trend graph
* Demand prediction (based on past data)


---

> The system will generate:
>
> 1. Operational Reports (active rentals, overdue bikes)
> 2. Financial Reports (daily/monthly revenue, profit estimation)
> 3. Bike Utilization Reports
> 4. Customer Behavior Reports
> 5. Inventory & Maintenance Reports
> 6. Receipts and Official Documents
> 7. Analytical Insights for decision making


---

> The goal is not just to record rentals, but to provide business intelligence to the shop owner to improve profitability, reduce losses, and optimize bike allocation.
